About Us

Who We Are

Founded in 1992, All Trades Mechanical Inc. is a leader in the mechanical contracting industry. Our team of service and installation experts handles projects of varying scope across multiple locations — including government facilities with full security clearance.

Who We Serve

Our customers include school districts, hospitals, federal government facilities, commercial office buildings, restaurants and bars, bank buildings, yacht clubs, county jails, correction facilities, catering facilities, bowling alleys, food banks, and commercial fishing boats.

Where We Work

We provide service throughout Kitsap, Mason, Pierce & King Counties. Our installation team takes on national projects as well, working with clients across the United States.
